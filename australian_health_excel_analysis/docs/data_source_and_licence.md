# Data source and licence

## Dataset title

Synthetic Australian Emergency Department Activity Dataset, 2024–2025

## Publisher

Created for this independent portfolio project. It is not published by, sourced from or endorsed by an Australian government or healthcare organisation.

## Source page and download location

The data are included directly in this repository:

- `data/raw/source_data.csv`
- `data/processed/cleaned_data.csv`

## Date created and accessed

3 August 2026

## Reporting period

1 January 2024 to 31 December 2025

## Geographic coverage

Fictional facilities across NSW, VIC, QLD, SA, WA, TAS, ACT and NT.

## File format

CSV and XLSX.

## Required attribution

Suggested attribution: “Synthetic Australian Emergency Department Activity Dataset, independent portfolio project, 2026.”

## Restrictions

The dataset must not be represented as actual health-service data or evidence of real Australian healthcare performance. Fictional facility names must not be mapped to real organisations.

The repository may include and redistribute the raw and cleaned synthetic files under the stated licence.
