# Assumptions and limitations

## Analytical assumptions

- Triage categories follow an Australasian-style five-level structure.
- Portfolio target thresholds are 0, 10, 30, 60 and 120 minutes for categories 1 to 5.
- A long ED stay is defined as more than 240 minutes for analytical illustration.
- Region types are simplified remoteness-style labels and are not official ASGS classifications.
- Each encounter is counted once after duplicate removal.
- Missing wait values are excluded from wait-based denominators and are not imputed.

## Source and coverage limitations

The data are synthetic. Facility names, encounter IDs and performance patterns are fictional. The dataset covers two calendar years and does not include staffing, bed occupancy, diagnostic turnaround, inpatient access block, socioeconomic measures, Indigenous status, comorbidity, detailed clinical outcomes or population denominators.

## Comparability and bias

Comparisons are not risk-adjusted. Differences may reflect synthetic case mix, facility scale, regional assumptions or random variation. Small jurisdictions and remote facilities have lower volumes, so percentage measures may be more volatile.

## Appropriate interpretations

The project can demonstrate Excel analytics, data validation, KPI design, operational reporting and descriptive healthcare analysis.

## Inappropriate interpretations

Do not use the results to assess a real facility, infer clinical quality, estimate actual Australian performance, claim causation, recommend clinical treatment, or support funding decisions without validated real data and appropriate governance.
