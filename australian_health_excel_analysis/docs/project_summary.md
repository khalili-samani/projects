# Project summary

## Problem

A healthcare operations stakeholder needs a concise view of emergency department demand, waiting-time performance, admissions, length of stay and geographic variation.

## Approach

A 12,079-row synthetic Australian ED dataset was cleaned, validated and analysed entirely in Microsoft Excel. The workbook includes structured tables, formula examples, quality checks, KPI definitions, summary tables, charts and an executive dashboard.

## Main findings

- Presentation volume increased 4.9% in 2025.
- The peak month was July 2025.
- Remote facilities had the lowest within-target performance.
- Mental health presentations had longer average ED stays than other groups in the synthetic scenario.
- The overall within-target rate was 44.7%; the admission rate was 29.0%.

## Recommendations

Investigate peak-period staffing and flow constraints, examine regional access differences with richer contextual data, review mental health pathways separately, strengthen source validation and add risk-adjustment variables before operational decisions.

## Skills demonstrated

Excel data cleaning, structured tables, lookup formulas, conditional logic, KPI development, data-quality assurance, healthcare interpretation, dashboard design, technical documentation and GitHub portfolio presentation.
